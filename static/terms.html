What Accent Changer is

Accent Changer is a web service that lets you record your speech and hear it played back with corrected English pronunciation, in a cloned version of your own voice. It is a practice and confidence tool, not a medical or speech-therapy service.

The one big rule: only your own voice

You may only clone your own voice. Cloning another person's voice without their permission is strictly forbidden, may be illegal where you live, and will result in immediate account termination. By ticking the consent box before cloning, you confirm the recording is of your own voice.

Your account

  • One free trial per person — creating multiple accounts to obtain extra free usage is not allowed.
